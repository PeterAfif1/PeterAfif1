## Projects
- **[Foreman](https://github.com/PeterAfif1/foreman)**: AI intake system for contractors. Turns messy emails, texts, and call notes into structured leads, with a tool-calling chat agent, reply drafts, and RAG estimates from past quotes. Eval suites with a CI gate cut extraction hallucinations from 5.9% to 1.4%.
- **[Backbeat](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6792944827)**: iOS app for drummers to create, practice, and track grooves and rudiments, with an AI groove generator, a 16-step sequencer, and practice analytics. Live on the App Store.
- **[JobAgent](https://github.com/PeterAfif1/JobAgent)**: AI agent that scores resume-to-job fit, finds skill gaps, and rewrites weak bullets in a LangGraph loop until it hits a target score.
